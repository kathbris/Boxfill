    Rule 12-3034 at a glance

    1. (1) Count each insulated conductor that enters or leaves and ends at a terminal or connector. A conductor running through uncut counts once. A conductor that starts and ends inside the box is not counted.
    2. (2) Table 23 limits, reduced by: one for each fixture stud or hickey, one for each pair of insulating-cap connectors, two for each flush device on a single strap.
    3. (3) A device more than 2.54 cm from strap to back uses extra space: 32 cm³ for each centimetre of its depth.
    4. (4) Boxes not in Table 23, or mixed wire sizes, use Table 22 volumes, with the same Subrule (2) allowances.
    5. (5) and (6) Usable space is the internal volume. Ganged sections and marked rings add their marked volumes.

    This is a study summary, not the rule text. Your 2024 codebook and Appendix B decide.
